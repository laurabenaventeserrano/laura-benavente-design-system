A work or lab card: 1px prism border, page fill, 2px radius, 16px padding, media on the panel colour.

## The consumer provides
`media` (an Imagery asset url) and `mediaAlt`, `kicker` (mono), `title` (serif), body as children, `meta` (mono), and `href` to make the whole card a link.

## Do
- Lay cards in the 12-column grid with the 32px `grid-gap`.
- Keep media 4:3 on `bg-panel`; product shots are reconstructions and the caption should say so.

## Don't
- No shadow, no hover lift, no scale. Depth comes from the line.
- Title size and body size are not fixed by the site; the card uses `heading-s` at its smallest (22px) and 15px body. Change them only together across all cards.
