The favicon mark: a prism ring (stroke 2.5 on a 32 grid) with a dot inside (radius 5 on 32).

## The consumer provides
`size` in px (16, 32 or larger) and an optional `label`.

## Notes
- The dot follows `text-primary`, so it is black on light and light on dark.
- The ring radius is drawn to touch the 32 box edge; the site states only the stroke and dot. `assets/Mark/favicon-16x16.png` is the real shipped favicon.
- One of the few round things in the system (`radius-round`), with the cursor.
